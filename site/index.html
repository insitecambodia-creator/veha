    Questions owners ask us

    Can my whole team use it?

    Yes. VEHA supports multiple user accounts, so each cashier can log in and run their own shift. Ask us about any limits on devices or branches for your plan.

    Can I use the equipment I already have?

    Often, yes. The Cloud POS License is for shops with compatible Android POS hardware. Tell us your terminal and printer models and we will confirm before you buy.

    How do I take payments?

    VEHA records cash and the payment methods you set up. For specific bank, KHQR or card options, and for mixed payments or currencies, ask us for a live demo so we can show exactly what works today.

    What happens in the free trial?

    You get 10 days with the full software and no payment required: products, discounts, split bills, modifiers, reports and the cloud back office.

    Is it easy for new staff to learn?

    The interface is built around the steps cashiers already do: pick a drink, choose options, take payment. We also provide tutorials and onboarding in Khmer.
