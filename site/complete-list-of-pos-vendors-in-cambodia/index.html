Cambodia’s point of sale (POS) software market has grown rapidly in recent years, driven by the expansion of cafés, restaurants, retail shops, minimarts, and service businesses across the country. Today, business owners searching for a POS system in Cambodia can choose from a wide range of local and international vendors, each offering different features, pricing models, hardware options, and levels of support. This page provides an overview of the POS vendors operating in Cambodia to help businesses better understand the available solutions and compare their options.

POS systems in Cambodia generally fall into several categories. Some vendors focus on cloud-based POS software designed for small and medium businesses, offering features such as sales tracking, product management, reports, and multi-user access. Others provide more advanced enterprise or ERP-oriented systems that integrate accounting, inventory, and business management tools. There are also POS providers that specialize in restaurant operations, including table management, kitchen display systems, QR ordering, and split billing, while retail-focused systems often prioritize barcode scanning, stock control, and fast checkout.

Hardware support is another important differentiator among POS vendors in Cambodia. Certain providers offer software-only solutions that run on existing Android devices or tablets, while others supply complete POS hardware packages that include touchscreen terminals, receipt printers, cash drawers, and customer displays. Some vendors promote all-in-one POS machines designed for high-volume environments, whereas others focus on affordable setups for small shops and startups.

Pricing models vary widely across POS systems in Cambodia. Some vendors charge a one-time license fee, while others operate on monthly or annual subscription plans. Inclusions such as software updates, customer support, cloud access, and additional modules can differ significantly, making it important for businesses to carefully review what is included in each offering. Language support, especially Khmer language compatibility, and local customer assistance are also key considerations for many Cambodian businesses.

By listing POS vendors available in Cambodia in one place, this page aims to help business owners research the local POS landscape more efficiently. Whether you are opening a new café, upgrading from manual bookkeeping, or switching from an existing POS system, understanding the range of vendors and solutions available is an essential first step toward choosing the right POS system for your business needs.
